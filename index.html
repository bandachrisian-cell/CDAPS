          🌀 Typhoon

          A tropical storm with strong winds and heavy rain that can cause floods and landslides.

          Did you know? Typhoons bring three dangers at once: strong wind, heavy rain, and storm surge along the coast.

          Know the signs: Official storm signals, a darkening sky, rising winds, and rough seas.

          Before

          Pack your go-bag, secure windows and roofing, charge your phone, and know your evacuation center.

          During

          Stay indoors away from windows. Follow official alerts. Evacuate if told to.

          After

          Avoid floodwater and fallen wires. Check on neighbors and report damage.

          🌊 Flood

          Water rising over normal ground level, often after heavy rain or when rivers overflow.

          Did you know? Many flood deaths happen when people try to walk or drive through moving water.

          Know the signs: Hours of heavy rain, rising rivers, blocked drains, and muddy water in streams.

          Before

          Know high ground nearby. Keep documents in waterproof bags.

          During

          Move to higher ground. Turn off electricity. Never walk or drive through floodwater.

          After

          Boil drinking water if unsure it is safe. Clean and dry your home.

          🏚️ Earthquake

          Sudden shaking of the ground. Aftershocks can follow.

          Did you know? An earthquake can strike without warning, so the best protection is practicing drop, cover, and hold on.

          Know the signs: There is usually no warning. Learn the safe spots in every room before it happens.

          Before

          Secure heavy furniture. Plan a meeting point.

          During

          Drop, cover, and hold on. Stay away from glass and shelves. If outside, move to an open area.

          After

          Expect aftershocks. Check for injuries and gas leaks. Leave damaged buildings.

          🔥 Fire

          An uncontrolled fire in a home or building that spreads quickly.

          Did you know? Smoke is often more dangerous than flames, so stay low and get out fast.

          Know the signs: Smell of smoke, crackling sounds, a hot door, or a smoke alarm going off.

          Before

          Check wiring and stoves. Know two ways out of every room.

          During

          Get out and stay out. Crawl low under smoke. Call 911 once you are safe.

          After

          Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe.

          ⛰️ Landslide

          Soil and rocks sliding down a slope, often after long rain.

          Did you know? Landslides can follow days of heavy rain or an earthquake, even after the rain stops.

          Know the signs: New cracks in the ground, tilting trees or poles, muddy streams, and rumbling sounds.

          Before

          Watch for cracks in the ground and tilting trees. Know your evacuation route.

          During

          Move away from the slide path, uphill sideways if possible.

          After

          Stay away from the slope. Report broken utilities.

          🌋 Volcano

          Ash, gas, and lava from an active volcano. Ashfall and lahars can reach far beyond the crater.

          Did you know? Ashfall and lahars (mud flows) can reach communities far from the crater.

          Know the signs: A higher official alert level, rumbling, steaming, small earthquakes, or ash plumes.

          Before

          Know the danger zone and the official alert level. Keep masks and goggles ready.

          During

          Stay indoors, cover your nose and mouth, and avoid river channels where lahars flow.

          After

          Clear ash from roofs carefully, since it is heavy when wet.

          👨‍👩‍👧 Make a family emergency plan

          Do this once and everyone knows what to do.

          1. Pick a meeting place near home and another outside your area.

          2. Write down key phone numbers and keep a copy in each go-bag.

          3. Assign roles: who grabs the bag, who helps children or elders, who locks up.

          4. Practice the plan twice a year.
